# LAND: Thornhaven: The Tithe Night

> **Document Purpose:** Canon for Thornhaven. Every soul, storyline and scene in the Western Valley
> is built on this. It replaces the original premise (archived in `docs/archive/STORY_NARRATIVE_v1.md`).
> The wider world (`docs/WORLD_BIBLE.md`) is built on it too; where they disagree, this file wins.
> **Last Updated:** November 2026

---

## The hook, in one breath

Every autumn, Iron Hollow takes one person from Thornhaven, chosen by lot in the chapel. Nobody
taken has ever come back. You walk into the village on the night of the drawing, just as Elder
Mathias reads the name: **Elena**, the merchant's daughter. The Hollow comes to collect in **seven
days**.

Everyone in the village now wants something from you, and they all want different things.

---

## What makes this story work (design rules)

1. **A clock from minute one.** Collection Night is a fixed point: it happens on day 7 whether or
   not the player is ready. The player shapes *how* it goes, not *whether*.
2. **A person at stake, not "the village".** Elena, with a name, a face and a cough.
3. **The mystery has a heart, not a culprit.** Who rigged the lot is the question. The answer is a
   person you love, and the reason breaks your heart.
4. **Everyone drives.** Each main NPC has something to win or lose by the Tithe and acts on it,
   whether or not the player asks.
5. **Dramatic irony, then reversal.** The player learns early that the lot is rigged and that the
   taken may be alive. The village learns later. Mira's truth comes last.

## The secret history (what really happened)

**Five years ago** the Grey Fever came back to the valley. Gregor's wife Lysa caught it, and so did
their daughter Elena, then thirteen. Fever-root eased them but couldn't break it.

A widow from the tavern, Mira, frightened, and "only a messenger", brought Gregor word that the
Hollow had a medicine: a white mineral salt that, steeped with fever-root, cures the Grey Fever
outright. The price was Gregor's help with something the Boss called *the Tithe*.

The Tithe was announced that winter: one villager a year, chosen by lot, in exchange for "peace".
The council, terrified, agreed. **Gregor's part was to rig the lot.** Bjorn had cast the lot tiles
from the forge's own iron. Gregor kept a duplicate set, and each year he palmed the draw so that
Elena's name never came up. In return the Hollow's salt kept coming.

The salt came too late for Lysa, who died that first winter. It has kept Elena alive ever since.
**Elena's life has been paid for by her neighbours' children.**

**The taken are not dead.** They are marched over the smugglers' ridge trail to the **Pit**: a
back-seam of Greystone's deep vein, which the Hollow broke into from the ridge side after Foreman
Dagna sealed her side six years ago. They dig the white salt in chains. The salt goes three ways:
- some to Gregor;
- most over the ridge to Greystone's carters;
- from there to the Guild and the Cathedral, which sells it on as the rationed fever cure.

In other words, **the Capitol's mercy is dug by Thornhaven's stolen children.**

## This year's draw: who rigged it, and why

The village assumes Gregor's luck finally ran out, or that the Hollow is punishing him. The truth:

**Elena rigged the lot against herself.**

This summer, Elena found her father's duplicate tiles in his strongbox, beside a packet of the white
salt she takes every night. She worked it out. She watched him palm the draw last year, and saw
Willa Brand's mother scream in the chapel. This year Gregor's palmed tile carried **Ada Fenn's**
name. Elena swapped it for her own.

She told no one. She means to go:
- so that no one else's child dies in her place;
- because the salt proves the taken are alive somewhere, and she means to find them;
- because she would rather walk into the dark than live on her neighbours' bones.

**Mira saw her do it** and let it stand. Elena in the Hollow's hands is the perfect leash on Gregor,
who would do anything now. She is the perfect prize to dangle before Varn, who has grown hungry. She
is also the perfect test of who in Thornhaven will finally fight.

## The seven days (the clock)

The game clock starts on **Day 1, 20:00, at the drawing**. **Collection Night is Day 7, 22:00**, at
the village gate. Day flags `tn_day_2` … `tn_day_7` turn true as each morning comes. This is a world
fixed point: the cart comes.

### Act I: The Lot (Days 1-2)
- **The drawing.** The player sees the chapel, Elena's name and Gregor's face. Mathias blesses it.
  Mira comforts Gregor. Bjorn stares at the tiles.
- **Three people ask the player for three incompatible things within the first day:**
  - Gregor asks them to get Elena out of the valley, or to find someone to go instead.
  - Aldric asks them to stand at the gate.
  - Elena asks them to make sure the lot stands, and to follow the cart.
- **Bjorn says the tile is wrong.** He cast them and knows their weight, and Elena's tile has been
  re-cut. The lot was rigged.
- **Act I ends** when the player knows the lot was rigged (`tn_lot_rigged_known`).

### Act II: The Rot (Days 3-5)
- **Who rigged it?** Suspicion falls in every direction:
  - on Gregor, who ought to have rigged it the other way;
  - on Mira, who has been seen near the chapel;
  - on Mathias, who keeps the urn.
- **The duplicate tiles** in Gregor's strongbox reveal five years of rigging. The village turns on
  him, or the player keeps the secret.
- **Elena's confession.** Trust or affection, or catching her, brings out the truth: she did it
  herself, and why.
- **Davin.** Old Maud's hut, the white stone, proof that the taken live. The question changes from
  "who goes" to "where do they go".
- **Bjorn learns his apprentice was taken by Gregor's rigging.**
- **Choices pile up:**
  - fight at the gate;
  - bargain with the tribute fund;
  - send a substitute;
  - follow the cart to the Pit;
  - expose everything to the village, or keep it quiet.
- **Act II ends** when the player commits to a plan for Collection Night.

### Act III: Collection Night (Days 6-7)
- **The gate.** Varn's cart, and whatever Thornhaven chose:
  - steel and farmers;
  - coin;
  - a different face in the cart;
  - Elena walking out herself.
- **Varn's move.** If Varn sees a chance, he takes Elena for himself, and the Hollow splits.
- **The Pit (optional Act III road).** The ridge trail from Iron Hollow to the deep vein, to free
  Corin and Willa, alone or with whoever the player has won over.
- **The Boss.** Only here, and only if earned, does Mira's truth surface: at the gate when the plan
  threatens the Pit, or in the Pit's tally book in her hand, or in Aldric's sealed account of the
  night Marcus died.

## Canon kept from the original

- Gregor supplies the Hollow, and Bjorn's steel reaches it through Gregor's orders. That is still
  true, alongside the Tithe.
- Mira is the Boss, behind the widow's mask. Marcus was eliminated when he learned too much, and Varn
  did it.
- Elena carries her mother's Grey Fever.
- The council's tribute fund sits in the chapel undercroft. The Tithe takes coin as well as a person.
- Aldric has an unsent martial-law proclamation. He trusts Mira.
- Bjorn's maker's mark is a hammer over a pine tree. He also cast the lot tiles.

## What changed

- The core is no longer "who feeds the bandits?" It is **the Tithe**: a person a year, rigged by
  Gregor, and this year rigged by Elena against herself.
- The old ledger is now the record of Gregor's rigged draws: the year, the tile palmed, the name it
  fell to. It sits beside his deliveries to the Hollow.
- The taken live, in the Pit. This ties Thornhaven directly to Greystone, the white salt, the Grey
  Fever cure and the Capitol.
- There is a new main NPC, **Davin**: Bjorn's apprentice, escaped from the Pit, hiding at Old Maud's
  hut.

## Canon details (settled in the souls; binding)

- **Who chose the names.** Mira, as the "messenger", sent each year's name to Gregor folded inside the
  salt packet. In Year 3 the slip said only *choose*. That was her test, and Gregor chose Hesk Marlow,
  because Hesk kept asking who held the urn's key. Hesk is Gregor's own private guilt.
- **The duplicate tiles.** Gregor paid Bjorn's apprentice **Davin** a dozen copper to pour a spare set
  from the leftover iron while Bjorn was at market. Bjorn never knew. A year later the lot Gregor
  rigged with those tiles took Davin himself.
- **The swap.** The evening before the drawing, in the chapel vestry, Elena took Ada Fenn's tile from
  her father's coat. She put in its place one of the duplicates, with its name filed off and her own
  cut in. That made it lighter, which is how Bjorn can tell it was recut. She threw Ada's tile down
  the well. Mira watched from the back pew. **Bjorn's true Elena tile is still in the urn.**
- **Ages and dates.** Elena is 18. Marcus died three autumns ago, following the Year 2 cart that
  took Davin. The Pit's tally book is in Mira's own hand.
- **Mira's `harm:elena`.** In Mira's eyes, taken is not harmed: taken is *kept*. Options in which the
  lot stands or Elena rides the cart are not tagged `harms: [elena]`.
